Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

1. Overview

This privacy policy applies to the Marked mobile application ("the App"). The App is designed as a privacy-first, offline journaling and habit-tracking tool.

2. Data Collection and Storage

Marked operates entirely offline. The App does not collect, transmit, harvest, or store any personal data, journal entries, or user information on external servers. All data created within the App is stored securely and locally on your personal device.

3. Peer-to-Peer Synchronization

If you choose to use the synchronization feature, the App uses a direct peer-to-peer connection to sync data between your own devices. Your journal entries are transferred directly over your network. Data is never routed through, or stored on, any third-party or developer servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs. We do not track your behavior or share your data with advertisers.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
